## 1. Problem and opportunity

**Objectiv** — one workspace for code, AI, and work conversations. **A-Zing Innovations.**

Software teams run their day across many separate tools: an editor or IDE, Slack or Teams, email, video meetings, tickets, and documentation. Each switch breaks focus; studies and team feedback consistently point to **hours lost per developer per week** to context switching alone. AI coding assistants have improved how people write code inside the editor, but they do not sit where **work conversations** happen, and companies are rightly cautious about AI that could **send messages without a human approving** them first.

At the same time, remote and hybrid work is normal. Chat and video are primary work surfaces, yet they are rarely integrated with how software is actually built. Companies pay **overlapping subscriptions** (IDE extensions, chat, mail, meetings) and still lack a single place where developers work **and** where leadership can enforce **approve-before-send** for AI-assisted communication. Objectiv addresses that gap: a **capital-efficient** product category (unified developer workspace with governed AI) at a moment when buyers are actively looking for safer AI adoption, not more siloed point tools.

## 2. What Objectiv is and launch readiness

**Objectiv** is a desktop **developer portal**: write and ship code in a familiar multi-project workspace; use the **Objective** AI assistant in the same window; and handle **chat, email, and meetings** without living in a browser tab farm. For companies, the model is deliberate: AI may **draft** replies and updates, but a person **approves** before anything goes to a client, channel, or inbox.

**Engineering and core development are largely complete.** The product is **in beta today**: multi-project desktop app (Mac first; Windows on roadmap); **Slack** in-product (channels, DMs, send, edit, reactions, offline cache, real-time Socket Mode); **meetings** inside the app; **Objective** coding agent (pilot). **Plan:** **public v1.0 launch in four months** (~February 2027 from a Q4 2026 start), with enterprise tier and approve-to-send ready. Months 1–3: design-partner beta and release polish; **month 4: go-live**. Funding prioritises **marketing and sales** to win paying teams after launch. Live demo available on request.

## 3. Market, pricing, and go-to-market

**Who pays:** software organisations roughly **20–200 people**—Indian SaaS companies, product engineering units inside IT services, GCCs, and Slack-heavy startups where tool sprawl is visible to the CTO. **Buyers** are typically the CTO, VP Engineering, or IT head; the pain is fewer tools, safer AI, and developers who stay in flow.

**How we charge:** free **community** tier for individuals; **enterprise** at **₹2,499 per seat per month** (annual contracts available); later, extensions marketplace share. At list price, a **30-seat** team is about **₹9 lakh per month** in subscription revenue.

**Go-to-market (50% of budget):** **months 1–3** — design-partner beta plus pre-launch brand and pipeline; **month 4** — public launch; **months 5–24** — founder-led enterprise sales, case studies, events, and seat expansion. The product is built; this plan buys **distribution and revenue**.

## 4. Competition, differentiation, and de-risking

**Alternatives** each cover one slice. VS Code, Cursor, and similar tools excel at editing but do not unify chat, meetings, and **governed** outbound messaging. Slack and Teams own conversation but are not the developer’s primary workspace for code and project context. Large suites are often slow to adopt and not optimised for **builder-first** daily use. Objectiv’s wedge is **one focused desktop app**: code, AI on the codebase, and the channels teams already use—with **human approval** before send.

**Why this is de-risked:** **core development is largely done**; the raise funds **launch and GTM**, not a long build cycle. Slack, meetings, and agent wiring are in place. **28%** of spend stays on product (hardening and maintenance); **50%** on sales and marketing. Monthly funding: **₹4L month 1**, then **₹2L/month** (**₹50L total**).
