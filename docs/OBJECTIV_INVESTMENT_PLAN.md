# Objectiv — 2-year investment plan

**A-Zing Innovations · Confidential · Currency: INR**

**What we’re building**  
Objectiv is a **single desktop app** where developers **write code**, use an **AI assistant**, and handle **work chat, email, and meetings** without jumping between Slack, Zoom, Outlook, and separate coding tools.

**Why it matters**  
Teams pay for many subscriptions and lose time switching apps. Objectiv **saves time**, **reduces tool sprawl**, and lets companies **control how the AI acts on behalf of employees** (approve before send).

**Revenue model**  
- **Community** — free for individual developers  
- **Enterprise** — **₹2,499 / seat / month** (annual contracts available)  
- **Extensions marketplace** — optional partner revenue share (later)

**Product status:** **Engineering and core development are largely complete** (beta live: Slack, meetings, agent path). The plan funds **launch polish**, **go-to-market**, and **sales**—not a multi-year build. **Public v1.0 launch at month 4** (~February 2027 from a Q4 2026 start).
